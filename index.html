FAQs

How do I place an order?

Tap Order Now on any cake — it opens WhatsApp with your cake pre-filled. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 84362 73886.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes! All our flavours can be made 100% eggless at no extra cost. Just mention it on WhatsApp while ordering.

Can I get a custom photo or theme cake?

Absolutely! Share your design/photo on WhatsApp at least 48 hours in advance and we'll craft it for you.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Gholdigrui, Pursurah, Champadanga, Arambagh, Tarakeswar and nearby areas of Hooghly.
